Our Process

Our Screening Process

A rigorous, multi-stage process to ensure you get the best candidates

01

CV Screening

We review every CV thoroughly, assessing qualifications, experience, and career progression against your requirements.

02

Initial Phone Interview

We conduct preliminary phone interviews to assess communication skills, motivation, and cultural fit.

03

Skills Assessment

We administer tailored skills assessments to verify technical competencies and job-specific abilities.

04

In-Depth Interview

We conduct comprehensive interviews to evaluate experience, problem-solving abilities, and alignment with your culture.

05

Reference Checks

We contact previous employers to verify work history, performance, and professional reputation.

06

Final Vetting

We conduct a final review to ensure all candidates meet your specific requirements before presenting them to you.
